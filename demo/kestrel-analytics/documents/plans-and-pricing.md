# Kestrel Analytics plans and pricing

Kestrel Analytics is a fictional web analytics service used for this demo project. All prices are in euros per month, excluding VAT.

| Plan | Monthly price | Events per month | Team seats | Data retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 0 | 10,000 | 1 | 30 days |
| Starter | 19 | 100,000 | 3 | 12 months |
| Team | 79 | 1,000,000 | 10 | 24 months |
| Business | 199 | 10,000,000 | 50 | 60 months |

## Events above the limit

Each plan includes the number of events shown in the table. Above the limit, Starter and Team are charged 0.40 euros for every additional 10,000 events. Business is charged 0.25 euros for every additional 10,000 events. The Free plan stops collecting events at its limit and is never charged.

Usage is counted per calendar month. Unused events do not roll over to the next month.

## Annual billing

Paying for a whole year gives a discount of 20 percent compared with monthly billing. With annual billing the Starter plan costs 182.40 euros per year, the Team plan costs 758.40 euros per year and the Business plan costs 1,910.40 euros per year.

## Education and non-profit discount

Verified universities, schools and registered non-profit organisations receive 50 percent off any paid plan. The discount applies to the organisation, not to individuals. Individual students are not covered by it; the Free plan is available to everyone.

## Trial

Team and Business start with a 14-day trial that does not require a credit card. Starter and Free have no trial period.

## Cancellation and refunds

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and end with the current billing month. Annual plans are refunded in full when cancelled within the first 30 days. After that, the remaining months are not refunded.
