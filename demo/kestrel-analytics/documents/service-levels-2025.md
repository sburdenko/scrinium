# Service levels (valid until 28 February 2026)

This version of the service level agreement applied during 2025 and until 28 February 2026. It was replaced by the 2026 version on 1 March 2026.

## Availability

Paid plans are covered by a monthly availability target of 99.5 percent. The Free plan has no availability target.

## Support response times

Support requests are answered within 48 hours on working days, on every paid plan. The Free plan is served through the community forum only.

## Service credits

If the monthly availability falls below the target, the customer receives a credit of 5 percent of that month's fee.
