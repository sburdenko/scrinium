# Service levels (effective 1 March 2026)

This version replaces the 2025 service level agreement and applies from 1 March 2026.

## Availability

| Plan | Monthly availability target |
|---|---|
| Free | no target |
| Starter | 99.5 percent |
| Team | 99.9 percent |
| Business | 99.95 percent |

## Support response times

| Plan | First response |
|---|---|
| Free | community forum only |
| Starter | within 48 hours on working days |
| Team | within 24 hours on working days |
| Business | within 4 hours, around the clock |

## Service credits

If the monthly availability falls below the target, the customer receives a credit of 10 percent of that month's fee for every 0.1 percentage point below the target. The credit is capped at 50 percent of the monthly fee.

## Maintenance

Planned maintenance is announced at least 5 days in advance and is not counted as downtime when it takes place between 02:00 and 04:00 central European time.
