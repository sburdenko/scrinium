# Data retention and privacy

## Where the data lives

Kestrel Analytics processes and stores all customer data in data centres in Frankfurt, Germany. Data is not transferred outside the European Union.

## Tracking without cookies

The tracking script does not set cookies and does not store anything on the visitor's device. IP addresses are shortened immediately on receipt and the full address is never written to disk. Because of this, most customers do not need a cookie banner for Kestrel, but this remains the customer's own legal assessment.

## Retention periods

Events are kept for a period that depends on the plan. After that period they are deleted automatically.

| Plan | Retention |
|---|---|
| Free | 30 days |
| Starter | 12 months |
| Team | 24 months |
| Business | 60 months |

Aggregated statistics, such as monthly visitor totals, are kept for the lifetime of the account.

## Deleting data

A visitor's data can be deleted through the API with `DELETE /visitors/{id}` or by writing to the support team. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Backups are encrypted and are kept for 35 days, after which deleted data disappears from them as well.

## Subprocessors and agreements

Kestrel uses two subprocessors: Nordhost GmbH for hosting and Mailwave for sending email. A data processing agreement is available on request and is signed within 5 business days.

## Incidents

In the case of a personal data breach, customers are notified within 48 hours of Kestrel becoming aware of it.
