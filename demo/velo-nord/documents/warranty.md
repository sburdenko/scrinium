# Warranty

Every repair comes with a six-month warranty on the labour. If the same fault comes back within six months, we fix it again without charging for the work.

New bikes have the statutory two-year warranty. On top of that, Velo Nord gives a five-year warranty on frames of the bikes it sells.

E-bike batteries are covered for two years or 500 charging cycles, whichever comes first.

Wear parts are not covered by any warranty. These are chains, cassettes, brake pads, tyres and handlebar tape.
