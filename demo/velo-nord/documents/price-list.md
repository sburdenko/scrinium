# Workshop price list

Velo Nord is a fictional bicycle shop and workshop in Hamburg, made up for this demo project.

These prices apply from 1 March 2026. All prices are in euros and include VAT.

| Service | Price | Typical time |
|---|---|---|
| Basic service | 59 | 1 day |
| Full service | 119 | 2 days |
| Brake bleed, per brake | 35 | 1 hour |
| Wheel truing | 25 | 30 minutes |
| E-bike diagnostics | 49 | 1 hour |

Parts are not included in these prices and are charged separately. For e-bikes, every service costs 20 euros more because the motor and battery are checked as well.

A basic service covers gears, brakes, tyre pressure and a safety check. A full service adds cleaning the drivetrain, truing both wheels and checking all bearings.
