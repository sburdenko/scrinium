# Opening hours

The shop is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:30 and on Saturday from 10:00 to 14:00. On Sundays and public holidays the shop is closed.

Bikes for the workshop can be dropped off until 17:00 on weekdays. On Saturdays the workshop takes no new repairs.

Between 24 December and 1 January the shop and the workshop are closed.

You find us at Lehmweg 12, 20251 Hamburg. The nearest underground station is Hoheluftbrücke.
