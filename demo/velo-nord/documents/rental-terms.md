# Rental terms

## Prices

A city bike costs 15 euros per day and an e-bike 39 euros per day. A weekly rental costs four times the daily price.

## Deposit and identification

Every rental needs a deposit of 200 euros, or 500 euros for an e-bike, and a valid identity card or passport.

## Return

Bikes are due back by 18:00 on the last day of the rental. A late return costs 10 euros for every started hour.

## Damage

The renter pays for damage caused during the rental, up to the amount of the deposit. Normal wear is not charged.
